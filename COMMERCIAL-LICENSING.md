# Commercial licensing

Commercial use of this repository, its production pipeline, or its bundled animation assets requires a paid license from HowdyDooToYou.

This page is a product and pricing overview, not a license grant. Commercial rights begin only when both parties accept a written order or license agreement.

## Launch plans

| Plan | Indicative annual price | Designed for |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Creator | $249 | One developer creating commercial animations for their own business or up to five client projects per year |
| Studio | $999 | Up to ten team members, unlimited internal and client projects, and production support priority |
| Product / OEM | From $4,800 | Embedding the runtime or pipeline in a product, hosted service, platform, template system, or customer-facing generator |

Volume, enterprise, education, and custom-support terms are available by agreement. Taxes and provider/API charges are not included.

## What a commercial license can include

- Production and internal business use.
- Commercial use of animation assets generated while the license is active.
- Client-delivery rights for rendered or exported animation assets.
- Updates released during the subscription term.
- Product embedding, redistribution, or hosted-generation rights when explicitly included in a Product / OEM order.

Unless a signed order says otherwise, a commercial license does not permit reselling, relicensing, or offering the pipeline itself as a competing standalone product.
