# Trademark policy

The MotionProof name, wordmark, and visual identity identify the project
maintained by HowdyDooToYou. The open-source licenses cover the software, not
those brand assets.

You may:

- truthfully state that your product uses or is compatible with MotionProof;
- link to the official repository; and
- retain required notices in copies and derived distributions.

Please do not:

- imply that a fork, service, plugin, or commercial product is official,
  endorsed, or maintained by MotionProof;
- use the MotionProof name as the primary name of a competing distribution or
  hosted service; or
- use a confusingly similar wordmark or visual identity.

Forks and modified distributions should choose their own primary name and
clearly identify the upstream MotionProof version they derive from.

This policy is not intended to restrict fair, nominative, or legally protected
uses of the name.
