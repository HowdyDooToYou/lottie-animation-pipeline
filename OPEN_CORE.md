# Open core and future premium offerings

MotionProof Core is free, open-source software under your choice of the MIT
License or Apache License 2.0. No paid license is required for commercial use,
client work, internal production, product embedding, hosting, modification, or
redistribution of the core software. Follow the license you select and preserve
the notices it requires.

## What is in the open core

The public repository and npm distribution include:

- the typed SDK and provider interface;
- the JSON CLI;
- the local MCP server;
- the Agent Skill plus Claude Code and Codex plugin manifests;
- deterministic, zero-key starter recipes;
- strict Lottie and browser-render certification;
- atomic five-artifact production bundles; and
- the local product studio.

The core is deliberately useful on its own. It is not a demo, a time-limited
trial, or a source-available shell around a proprietary runtime.

## What may become paid

If the project earns adoption, separately distributed offerings may include:

- larger curated motion and industry recipe packs;
- private brand-system packs and team recipe registries;
- hosted generation, collaboration, review, and asset history;
- managed provider routing and organization controls;
- priority support, implementation help, warranties, and service levels; and
- OEM integration, custom certification policy, and enterprise deployment.

No premium pack or hosted service is for sale yet. The first signal is demand:
use the core, star the repository, open useful issues, and tell us what would
save your team meaningful production time.

## Rights do not move backward

A version released under `MIT OR Apache-2.0` remains available under that
choice. A later commercial offering cannot revoke or narrow those existing
rights. Premium code, content, services, warranties, and support can carry
separate terms because they are separate offerings.

## Generated outputs

MotionProof does not claim ownership of the prompt you provide or the bundle it
creates for you. Built-in deterministic recipes are distributed as part of the
open core under the same dual-license choice.

When you connect a model or other provider, that provider's terms and the rights
in any material you supply still apply. You are responsible for confirming that
you may use and distribute imported candidates, fonts, images, sounds, and other
inputs. MotionProof's certified subset rejects external media and font
dependencies, but certification is not an intellectual-property clearance.
